“Spotto!”

The car game that keeps score

Somebody spots a yellow car and calls it. Spotorama awards the point, remembers who is winning, and settles the argument before it starts.

Where this came from

Spotto is one of those games nobody is taught and everybody knows. You are in the back of a car, somebody shouts “Spotto!” at a yellow car, and that is the whole rulebook. It has been played on Australian roads for generations, and every family plays it slightly wrong in their own particular way.

The trouble has always been the scoring. Somebody calls a yellow car and somebody else says they called it first. Somebody claims a ute that was more mustard than yellow. By the third hour nobody can remember whether it was 14–11 or 11–14, and the person who was winning is very sure it was the former.

Spotorama is the referee. Everyone gets a colour and a shape, you call what you spot, and the phone keeps the score properly — including the things families argue about most, like what a spot is worth and what actually counts as winning.

What it does

Up to seven players

One phone, everyone in the car. Each player gets a colour and a shape, and the shape stays theirs everywhere so you can find yourself at a glance.

Call it or tap it

Say “Spotto!” and the app hears it, or tap the card. Voice is optional and every spoken action can be tapped instead.

Your rules, not ours

Edit any list, change what a spot is worth, invent your own, and choose how a game ends — on time, on points, or when somebody finishes the list.

Teams

Kids against grown-ups, front seat against back. The app can suggest a fair split, or you can pick.

Works with no signal

Built for the middle of nowhere. No account, no server, nothing to sign in to — everything stays on your phone.

Every trip remembered

Past trips are kept with the full scoreboard, so you can settle who actually won the drive to the coast in April.
